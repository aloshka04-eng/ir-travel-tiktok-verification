Privacy Policy

IR Travel Content. Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information IR Travel Content reads from TikTok and how that information is used. The tool is operated by IR Travel, an Israeli travel agency owned by Irina Kuperman.

1. Scope

The app is used only for IR Travel's own TikTok account, @ir.travel. It does not collect data from other TikTok users.

2. Information we access

After the account owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit, the app may read:

  • Basic profile information (TikTok permission: user.info.basic)
  • The account's own video list and basic performance metrics (TikTok permission: video.list)

The app does not request access to other users' accounts, videos, or personal data.

3. How we use information

This information is used only for the account owner's content management: to view the @ir.travel account, review existing videos and basic metrics, and prepare video drafts. All publishing is reviewed and approved manually by the account owner.

We do not sell this information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it, except with the processors named below as needed to operate the app, or if required by law.

4. Storage and deletion

Data is stored only as needed for the account owner's content management. It can be deleted upon request by contacting IR Travel using the details below.

5. Third-party processors

Composio, an integration platform, handles the OAuth connection between the app and TikTok. TikTok also processes login and account data under TikTok's own privacy policy.

6. Your control

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok account settings. After access is revoked, the app can no longer read the TikTok account through that connection.

7. This website

These legal pages are static informational pages. They do not set cookies and do not collect personal data from visitors.

8. Changes and governing law

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the effective date at the top of this page will change. This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Israel.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact IR Travel by phone or WhatsApp: 054-700-0595.
